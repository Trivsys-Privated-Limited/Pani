How much is a water tanker in DHA Karachi?

A 3,000 gallon tanker is Rs 12,500 and a 6,000 gallon tanker is Rs 22,000. The price is the same in every DHA phase and includes delivery.

How far in advance do I need to book?

At least 24 hours before your chosen slot. The booking form only shows slots that meet this rule.

Which DHA phases do you deliver to?

All of DHA Karachi: Phase 1, 2, 2 Extension, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 7 Extension and 8. We don't deliver outside DHA.

Which tanker size do I need?

Most houses and villas use a 3,000 gallon tanker. For large bungalows, buildings, pools and construction, the 6,000 gallon tanker is better value.

How do I pay for the water tanker?

By bank transfer. You'll see our account details when you book. Send the amount, take a screenshot and upload it. We confirm on WhatsApp once the payment is checked.

Can I order more than one tanker?

Yes. You can book up to 5 tankers in one order. For larger or regular deliveries, message us on WhatsApp.

Is tanker water safe to drink?

No. Pani delivers water for household use: cooking, cleaning, bathing, laundry, gardens and construction. For drinking, use a filter or bottled water.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your booking number at least 12 hours before your slot and we'll reschedule or arrange a refund.
